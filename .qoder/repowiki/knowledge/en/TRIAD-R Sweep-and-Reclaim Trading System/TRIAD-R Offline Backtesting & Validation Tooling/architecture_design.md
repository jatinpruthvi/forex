Flat collection of independent CLI scripts organized into two loosely coupled tiers with strict one-way dependency direction. The strategy exploration tier (`strategy_orb.py`, `strategy_optimizer.py`, `aggressive_optimizer.py`, `parameter_grid_search.py`, `extended_grid_search.py`, `multi_pair_grid_search.py`, `_validate_4yr.py`) is self-contained: each defines its own `Bar`/`TradeResult` dataclasses, DST/session helpers, M5/M15 bar builders, ATR(14), lot sizing via fixed 0.4% risk fraction, and nested-grid loops over module-level constant lists; they read tab-delimited tick CSVs from `validation/HistoryData/` and print leaderboards or write markdown findings with no cross-imports. The validation pipeline tier forms a strict producer/consumer chain: `tick_signal_builder.py` converts raw bid/ask ticks into an observed-event CSV; `replay_export.py` validates that event CSV against the frozen V2.1 schema (documented in its docstring as the missing producer) and expands it into full calendar coverage `ReplayRow`s by importing shared types (`ReplayRow`, `FillPolicy`, `CSV_FIELDS`, `ALLOWED_COMBINATIONS`) from `triad_validation`; `triad_validation.py` loads the registry, enforces coverage, applies a conservative fill policy, runs WALK_FORWARD/HOLDOUT simulations with block-bootstrap confidence bounds, and outputs a JSON report; `triad_ablation.py` layers a preregistered ablation round on top by reusing both `replay_export` and `triad_validation`. Dependency direction is strictly one-way: explorers import nothing; `triad_ablation` → `replay_export` → `triad_validation`; there are no imports from explorers into the validation tier.
